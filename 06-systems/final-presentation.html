The one insight

The drop isn't fewer signups, and it isn't a failing notification channel. It's a rising break rate among starters — a growing share of people who do start are gone by day 7.

2 · Why Now

The trend was already there, and week 1 is where it bites hardest

Day-7 retention by cohort week, pre-launch

Day-7 retention fell every cohort week, before the Comeback screen existed. The decline shows no sign of leveling off on its own.

Day-30 retention: broke in week 1 vs. sustained

Users who break their momentum in week 1 retain roughly half as well by Day-30 as users who make it past week 1, and churn 16 points higher. Across every research source, the same root cause surfaces: the reset is all-or-nothing and there's no way back in.

Sources: data/metric-findings.md, Q1 & Q2 · docs/decision-brief.md 2 / 6
3 · The Proposal

A personalized Comeback screen, not a general fix

What it is

  • Best-streak stat shown first, not a zeroed counter
  • A short comeback session to rebuild momentum, no pass/fail
  • A free, pre-applied streak-freeze — no tap, no catch

Built with data Streakly already has. No new integrations.

What it isn't

  • Not a fix for notification tone or frequency — that's a separate theme
  • Not monetized — the freeze is free by design, the white space no competitor has claimed
  • Not a committed, scoped build yet — this is discovery
Sources: docs/decision-brief.md, Option B & finding 4 · CLAUDE.md · change_log.md Entry 10 3 / 6

5 · The Plan

The test is designed. Three things still block a timeline.

Milestone reached

Full powered test designed

Sized to detect a 5-point lift at 80% power, 95% significance, based on the pilot's own result.

Risk of waiting

Every week without addressing the week-1 break locks in the same drop we're already fighting. The pilot's numbers show the gap compounding, not holding flat.

Still open

Eligible-user volume unconfirmed
Freeze eligibility rules undefined
Sprint kickoff date not scheduled

Test needs ~0.46% of 85,000 WAU/week to fit an 8-week window — that volume isn't confirmed yet.

Not in sources: a committed rollout timeline. Discovery is scoped as 8 weeks from kickoff, but kickoff itself isn't scheduled.
Sources: data/experiment-design.md, Step 4 · stakeholders/raj.md · docs/prd.md · CLAUDE.md, Known gaps 5 / 6
6 · The Ask

Sign off on the test, help unblock one number

The ask

Sign-off to run the full powered test before a company-wide scale decision, and a decision on the one number currently blocking that test's duration: real weekly eligible-user volume.

What this is not

Not a request to scale the feature today. The pilot result is real and directionally strong — 76% vs. 46% Day-7 — but n=50 per variant is not enough on its own to commit the whole company to.
