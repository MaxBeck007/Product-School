# Persona: Dax R., the Hard-to-Please Miniature Painter

> **Not from course-supplied research.** Priya, Tom, and Amara
> (`research/interview-synthesis.md`) came from interview excerpts given in
> the P2L1 exercise. Dax did not, this persona was constructed on 2026-09-30,
> right after the lesson track changed from guitar to miniature painting
> (`change_log.md` Entry 7), specifically to stress-test that change with a
> skeptical, hobby-fluent voice Priya/Tom/Amara can't provide. Treat every
> reaction attributed to him as a plausible projection from the traits stated
> below, not as real user research. Do not cite him to Marcus or Raj as if he
> were a real user.

## Profile

- **Name:** Dax R.
- **Age / role:** 29, network engineer.
- **Hobby context:** competitive tabletop wargamer, 10+ years painting
  competition-entry miniatures. Active in hobby Discord servers and painting
  critique threads.
- **Streakly usage:** on the miniature-painting track, using Streakly to hold
  a daily painting habit between tournaments. Broke a streak once before,
  the exact scenario this feature targets.

## Disposition

- **Hard to please, specifically about competence, not emotion.** Amara is
  anxious about the mechanic itself; Tom is skeptical about trust; Dax
  doesn't have either reaction; a broken streak doesn't wound him. His
  objection, if he has one, is whether the app actually understands the
  craft, or is cosplaying understanding of it.
- **Pushes back on:** twee or infantilizing copy, generic gamification
  language layered onto a craft he takes seriously, and praise that isn't
  earned, in his words, "participation-trophy energy."
- **Needs before trusting a feature:** hobby terminology used accurately
  (basecoat, highlight, wash, drybrush, edge highlighting are all distinct
  techniques, not interchangeable words), and a sense that "progress" means
  something specific to this hobby, not just a generic streak number reused
  from a language app.
- **Communication style:** direct, a little blunt, will name the exact word
  or claim he doesn't buy rather than a vague complaint.

## Standard 4 questions

1. What do you think this does?
2. How would you arrive at this experience?
3. Would you come back and restart your streak based on this?
4. If you had a magic wand, what would you change?
