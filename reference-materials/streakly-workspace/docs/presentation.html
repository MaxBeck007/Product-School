1 · The Problem

Day-7 retention is failing, and we know why

48% → 39%
Day-7 retention, before and after the v2 redesign

The one insight

The drop isn't fewer signups, and it isn't a failing notification channel. It's a rising break rate among starters: 38.9% → 56.5% across cohort weeks 1–4.

Sources: CLAUDE.md · docs/decision-brief.md · data/metric-diagnosis.md, section 1 · docs/recommendation-memo.md 1 / 6
2 · Why Now

The trend was already there, and week 1 is where it bites hardest

What changed

The v2 redesign shipped, and the pre-launch decline shows no sign of leveling off on its own: Day-7 retention fell every cohort week, 60% → 44%, before the Comeback screen existed.

What we learned

Users who break their momentum in week 1 retain roughly half as well by Day-30 (18.0%) as users who make it past week 1 (33.5%), and churn 16 points higher.

Across every research source, the same root cause surfaces: the reset is all-or-nothing and there's no way back in.

3 · The Proposal

A personalized Comeback screen, not a general fix

What it is

  • Best-streak stat shown first, not a zeroed counter
  • A 60-second lesson to rebuild momentum
  • A one-tap, free streak-freeze

Built with data Streakly already has. No new integrations.

What it isn't

  • Not a fix for notification tone or frequency, that's a separate theme
  • Not monetized, the freeze is free by design, that's the white space no competitor has claimed
  • Not a committed, scoped build yet, this is discovery
Sources: docs/decision-brief.md, Option B & finding 4 · CLAUDE.md 3 / 6

5 · The Plan

The test is designed. Three things still block a timeline.

Milestone reached

The full powered test is designed and ready to run: sized to detect a 5-point lift at 80% power, 95% significance, based on the pilot's own result.

Risk of waiting

Every week without addressing the week-1 break locks in the same drop we're already fighting. The pilot's numbers show the gap compounding, not holding flat.

Still open

  • Real weekly eligible-user volume is unconfirmed. Test needs ~0.46% of 85,000 WAU/week to fit an 8-week window.
  • Freeze eligibility and expiry rules are still undefined (Raj).
Not in sources: sprint kickoff date and a committed rollout timeline. Discovery is scoped as 8 weeks from kickoff, but kickoff isn't scheduled yet.
Sources: data/experiment-design.md, Step 4 · stakeholders/raj.md · docs/prd.md · CLAUDE.md, Known gaps 5 / 6
6 · The Ask

Sign off on the test, help unblock one number

The ask

Sign-off to run the full powered test before a company-wide scale decision, and a decision on the one number currently blocking that test's duration: real weekly eligible-user volume.

What this is not

Not a request to scale the feature today. The pilot result is real and directionally strong, but n=50 per variant is not enough on its own to commit the whole company to.
